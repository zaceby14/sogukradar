RollKraft measures the force acting on the rolled material within the rolling stand and provides the measured value for the mill control system. The measurement principle is based on strain gauge technology. The resulting signal change is converted into a force value by the IMS KraftController (MMV). This value is then made available for continuous process control and monitoring.
