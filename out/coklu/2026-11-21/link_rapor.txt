Read Aloud Government-run Steel Authority of India Limited's ( SAIL ) Bokaro Steel Plant (BSP) developed a special grade steel for the first time, claiming it to be a major step in expanding its portfolio of high-value products, a company statement said on Friday, November 20. The newly developed steel finds applications in electric motors, generators, small power transformers, electrical appliances, and rotors for hybrid and electric vehicles, contributing to improved energy efficiency, the company said. Under BSP's product development initiative, it successfully produced, 1,100 mt of 0.5 mm thick non-grain oriented (NGO) electrical steel for the first time, it said. The heats for this newly developed grade were produced through the Steel Melting Shop-II (SMS-II) route with a specially designed composition - low carbon content to ensure improved ductility and formability, and high silicon content to provide the magnetic properties required for electrical steel, it said. The slabs were rolled to 2.8 mm thickness and 1280 mm width in the Hot Strip Mill (HSM) and subsequently cold reduced to 0.5 mm thickness in the Cold Rolling Mill-3 (CRM-3) to achieve high precision, excellent surface finish, and consistent magnetic behavior, it said. With its uniform magnetic properties in all directions, it ensures efficient energy conversion and reduced losses, it added.
